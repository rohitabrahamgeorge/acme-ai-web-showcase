What is Acme AI

Busywork, handled end to end

Acme AI is the AI work platform for operations, finance, sales and support teams.

Private AI Models+

Models run in our cloud, your private cloud or on your own hardware, and your data is never used to train them.

Acme Relay+

The work engine. Relay picks up a request, gathers context from every connected tool, drafts the result with sources, and delivers it once the right person signs off.

Connectors+

The connection layer. Connectors bring context in from the tools you already use and carry finished work back out, so Acme AI fits your stack instead of replacing it.

Playbooks+

The way your best people handle a task becomes a versioned Playbook that Runner can execute and Scout can check, so know-how lives in the system rather than in one inbox.

Ten Work Modules+

Reports, Inbox, Tickets, Meetings, Docs, Spreadsheets, Follow-ups, Approvals, Research and Onboarding, each switched on independently.

Workshop+

Build, tailor and own your workflows in Workshop: your logic, your IP, in plain readable steps.

Vault Layer+

The privacy layer. It masks sensitive fields before anything reaches a model and keeps a full record of what was used, when and by whom.

Our story

The founding team

Operators turned builders

A handful of people who spent years buried in status reports, follow-ups and spreadsheets built the tool they kept wishing for. The three things that hurt most became three parts of the product.

Between them they had watched every generation of productivity software promise to save time, then quietly add one more tab to check. Their conclusion was not that the tools were bad. It was that nobody had ever given busy teams a single place where the routine work actually gets done.

Every one of them had also seen an automation fire at the wrong moment and create more work than it saved. So the rule was set early: anything Acme AI does on your behalf carries the same checks a colleague’s work would, and can be stopped by the same people.
