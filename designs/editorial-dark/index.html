Acme AI is an AI work platform for busy teams. At its centre is Acme Relay, an engine that picks up requests, gathers context from your tools, drafts the work and delivers it once the right person signs off. It is built for operations, finance, sales, support and people teams.

No. A chatbot answers questions; Acme AI finishes tasks. It reads from and writes to the tools you already use, follows your approval rules, and hands back completed work with every source attached.

No. Acme AI sits on top of your mail, docs, CRM, helpdesk and finance tools and makes them work together. Those tools keep doing what they do well.

Instead of another tool your team has to operate, Acme AI delivers the outcome: the report written, the queue cleared, the follow-up sent. Every action is scoped, approval-gated where your policy says so, reversible and logged.

Yes. Acme AI runs in our cloud, in your private cloud or on your own hardware, including the AI models. Your data is never used to train models.

Many teams already have a few scripts and Zaps, and Acme AI keeps them. But rule-based automations break the moment a request is slightly different. Acme AI handles the judgement in between, with approvals and a full history.

Your work keeps moving. Acme AI is a layer over your tools, not a replacement, so your team can always work directly. If you leave, every Playbook and workflow you built exports with you.

Nothing your people lack the skill for. It simply works across every tool at once, never loses context between tasks, and runs your best Playbooks at any hour.
